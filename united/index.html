United live data one click, nothing to install

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar (one time): Send to gate display
  2. Open United Flight Status and find your flight: it searches by route or by flight number.
  3. Click the bookmark. That's it: live times, delay, gate, boarding time, amenities, and the upgrade/standby lists with capacity land here.
  4. For the next departure from the gate, open departures on FlightView and click the same bookmark there.

Click it again any time to refresh. The data goes straight from your united.com tab to this page in your browser and is never uploaded. Copy link for another device puts the whole screen in the link, passenger names included, so treat that link as private. Open flight status page →

Find a flight optional: united.com's own search is usually quicker

Step 2 is normally the whole answer: united.com searches by route or by flight number itself. This is for when you would rather not leave this page — it asks FlightView which legs a flight number flies, or which flights fly a route that day, and fills the flight in here so the link in step 2 points straight at it. The local feed answers a lookup straight away; without it, FlightView opens in another tab and the same bookmark answers it there.

Local feed optional, keeps the screen up to date on its own

Shows the next UA flight in the calendar once it's within 5 hours; until then a random UA departure from a United hub in the next 2 hours. The link is kept only in this browser and on your computer, never in shared links.

Start the feed once on this computer: pip install playwright tzdata airportsdata, then python server/gate_feed.py. It follows a gate and rolls over to the next flight after departure, and remembers what you send it. Without it, use the bookmark steps above.
